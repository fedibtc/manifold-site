For Liquidity Providers

Supply the Lightning liquidity federations use

A federation runs on its own. When its members want to send and receive over Lightning, liquidity providers can supply that liquidity, and are paid for what they supply.

A financial role, not an operational one

You supply the liquidity and earn from what flows through it. Guardians and operators run the federation.

An open role

Anyone can take it. Fedi can supply liquidity to early federations, and independent providers can take the role as the network grows.

Built on trust

Trust the guardians, not a company

No Guardian controls the federation. Funds are protected by the number of guardians, so multiple independent guardians would need to fail or collude for funds to be at risk.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Manifold?
Manifold is an open protocol that helps you set up a federation for your community without running the servers yourself.
Who runs my federation day-to-day?
Guardians run it day-to-day. They are the key holders for your federation.
How do I know if the Guardians are trustworthy?
Every operator hosting a guardian seat is verified before they can take it. A real person has vouched for them, the credential can be checked by anyone, and it can be revoked if it no longer holds.
How do I pay for the setup?
You pay the guardians in ecash, from an eligible federation. The app shows which of your federations are eligible, and if your balance is short it guides you through topping it up before you pay.
What if an operator drops out?
During formation, the app proposes replacements for you to approve. Once live, the Guardians absorb downtime, which is exactly what the extra guardians are for.
